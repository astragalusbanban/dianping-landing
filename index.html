常见疑问FAQ

你可能想问You might be wondering

点仔是什么？在哪里找到它？What is Dianzai, and where do I find it?

点仔是大众点评首页右下角那只小熊猫。轻点它，选好今天的状态、人数和预算，再点「点仔帮我挑」，就能得到推荐。Dianzai is the red panda in the bottom corner of the Dianping home page. Tap it, choose your mood, group size and budget, then tap "Help me pick" to get your picks.

点仔推荐和普通搜索有什么不一样？How are Dianzai Picks different from search?

搜索适合你已经知道要找哪家店的时候。点仔适合你还没想好吃什么的时候：它会一步步问你，按你的口味挑出几家，并写明推荐每一家的理由。Search is for when you already know which place you want. Dianzai is for when you don't know what to eat yet: it asks you a few questions, then picks a few places for your taste and says why it chose each one.

它凭什么知道我爱吃什么？How does it know what I like?

它参考你的口味档案、你收藏过的店，以及你选的「最近状态」，比如想吃辣、想清淡、控卡中。碰到你忌口的菜，匹配度会自动降低。It uses your taste profile, the places you've saved, and the mood you pick, such as spicy, light or cutting calories. Dishes you can't eat lower the match score automatically.

我的口味变了怎么办？What if my taste changes?

你可以随时改「最近状态」，「控卡中」和「放纵一下」还能设置结束日期。吃完饭在首页点一下「很合」「太辣了」或「太淡了」，口味档案就会跟着更新。用得越多，点仔越懂你。You can change your mood whenever you like, and "cutting calories" or "treat myself" can be given an end date. After a meal, tap "just right", "too spicy" or "too bland" on the home page and your taste profile updates. The more you use it, the better Dianzai knows you.

「避雷」靠谱吗？会不会是假评论？Can I trust the 避雷 alerts? Could they be fake?

避雷摘要只统计近 30 天内有核销订单的人勾选的标签，每一条都同时写明比例和人数，你一眼就能看出问题是个别情况还是很多人都遇到。The 避雷 summary only counts tags from people with a redeemed order in the last 30 days. Each issue shows both the share and the head count, so you can see at a glance whether it's a one-off or a common problem.

和朋友吃饭，怎么用点仔一起决定？How do I decide with friends?

在店铺页点「加入备选投票」，最多选三家，然后分享到微信群。朋友在卡片里能看到每家的评分、人均和有没有位。选出来的那家可以直接预订。On a restaurant's page, tap "Add to vote" to add it to a vote, up to three places, then share the vote to your WeChat group. Friends can see each place's rating, price per person and table availability on the card, and you can book the winner straight away.

我的口味和评价会被别人看到吗？Can other people see my taste reviews?

不会。你写的口味评价按账号单独保存，只有你自己能看到。No. Your taste reviews are saved to your own account, and only you can see them.

现在用有什么优惠？Is there a launch offer?

第一次用点仔挑店或发起投票，送 20 元券（券码 FRIDAY20），本周日前有效，随时可退。Use Dianzai to pick a place or start a vote for the first time and get a ¥20 coupon (code FRIDAY20). It's valid until Sunday and refundable anytime.
